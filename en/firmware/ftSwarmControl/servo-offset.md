In this menu item you can set the center position of servos in the swarm. First select the desired servo:

![Home](/assets/img/ftSwarmControl/servooffset/servo.png){:.align-center style="width: 25%;"}

Now adjust the servo center position with **-** and **+**.

![Home](/assets/img/ftSwarmControl/servooffset/offset.png){:.align-center style="width: 25%;"}

Save the changes with **Save**.
